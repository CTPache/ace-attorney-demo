'''''Turnabout Don''''' is a case from the Joypolis ''Ace Attorney'' attraction. It features [[Phoenix Wright]] defending actress [[Desireé DeLite]] after she is framed for bludgeoning her co-star to death during a rehearsal inside a locked cage.

==Nov. 21==
=== The crime ===
During a rehearsal inside a high spiked cage at the Joypolis Colosseum, the renowned actress [[Yumi]] was murdered by bludgeoning. The true culprit was [[Don]], the executive producer and president of the "Don-ism" talent agency. When Yumi was left alone in the locked cage in a paralyzed, panicked state, Don utilized a ceiling hoist system—attaching it not to himself, but to the bottom of the cage fence—to lift the heavy panel and slip inside. He murdered her with a real glass vase painted with luminescent paint.

===Arrest===
[[Desireé DeLite]] had struck Yumi earlier with a safe prop vase as part of the rehearsal. Upon seeing the ensuing fake blood (which triggered Yumi's severe phobia), Desireé immediately left the cage using her designated hoist belt to call for an ambulance. When paramedics arrived, they found Yumi dead inside the auto-locked cage. Since supposedly only Desireé and Yumi possessed the belts required to enter and exit, Don accused Desireé on the spot, leading to her arrest. 

===Investigation===
[[Phoenix Wright]] arrived at the Joypolis Colosseum where Detective Gumshoe explained the murder occurred in a 5-meter-tall cage requiring a special hoist belt to enter and exit.

At the Detention Center, Phoenix met with [[Desireé DeLite]] (Mareka). She stated that Yumi had a severe phobia of the color red, meaning red clothes were banned. During rehearsal, Desireé struck Yumi's arm with a glow-in-the-dark prop vase, triggering Yumi's phobia when "blood" appeared. Desireé then left the cage via the hoist to call an ambulance. Desireé also revealed Don's ambition to take over Joypolis, which Yumi opposed, and that Yumi often spread malicious rumors, angering stagehand Sal Manella (Usaki).

In the Dressing Room, Phoenix confronted [[Don]], an arrogant former fighter weighing 150kg. Don claimed his "3.0 vision" allowed him to exactly see Desireé strike Yumi on the head with a real vase. During this conversation, Phoenix noticed multiple costumes and obtained the Missing Costume evidence. Don also warned Phoenix about wet marbled acrylic paint being used by Sal Manella for a sign.

In the Archive Room, Gumshoe confirmed that no fake blood was used in the play due to Yumi's phobia. When discussing the play's glowing vase, Phoenix realized that a glowing fragment could prove Desireé's story. Don momentarily appeared to intimidate Gumshoe and ominously hinted that a "troublesome board member" being gone would allow him to control Joypolis.

Inside the locked ring, Phoenix and Gumshoe entered using Desireé's belt with a special ceiling wire (140 kg weight limit). They discovered the murder weapon, a thick glass vase shattered strangely at the neck. Hidden in the debris, Phoenix found a glass shard (glowing fragment) pointing to the prop vase. On the floor was Yumi's discarded belt, which Phoenix noticed was stained with wet marbled paint. Finally, Phoenix examined the cage fence, noting its tight 10cm openings and observing a suspiciously bent portion of barbed wire.

==Nov. 22==
===Trial ===
In District Court No. 1, Prosecutor [[Miles Edgeworth]] argued that Desireé's exit was a calculated escape to commit murder with a real vase.

During the first cross-examination, [[Don]] testified that he perfectly witnessed the event with his "3.0 vision" and confidently claimed there was no prop vase at the crime scene. Wright dismantled this lie by presenting the **Glowing Fragment** located right near Don's feet in a crime scene photo, proving the prop vase was shattered.

Cornered, Don changed his story, testifying that he was merely doing paperwork and completely missed the details. He claimed that the stage director, [[Sal Manella]] (Usaki), was in the lighting booth and witnessed the murder.

Sal Manella took the stand and testified he watched from the booth and never entered the fenced area. However, Wright presented the **Belt** to point out a glaring contradiction: Sal's clothes were covered in the exact same wet marbled paint found on Yumi's discarded belt. Realizing he was caught, Sal confessed he was instructed to lie. In truth, Don pressured him to tell the panicked Yumi to "Come over here!", allowing Yumi to pass her belt through the cage mesh to him.

Don was recalled to the stand as the true culprit. Sweating but defiant, Don made his final claim: it was physically impossible to commit the crime because the ceiling hoist had a strict 140 kg (308 lbs) limit, while he weighed 150 kg (330 lbs). Wright delivered the final blow by presenting the **Fence** and pointing out the bent barbed wire. Wright argued that Don hooked Yumi's belt to the bottom of the fence mesh. Instead of lifting himself, Don used the hoist to lift the 30 kg fence panel itself and slipped underneath the gap. Completely defeated, Don suffered a massive breakdown as his "flawless plan" was exposed. Desireé was declared Not Guilty.

===Aftermath ===
Desireé DeLite was found Not Guilty. It was revealed that Don enacted the murder simply because he viewed human suffering and the judicial system as manipulable entertainment, framing Desireé in a theatrical display of his own "genius" to silence Yumi, who had a habit of spreading malicious rumors.
