## Incident Overview

On the evening of November 21st, the renowned actress Yumi was found dead during a rehearsal for "21st Century Champion," a futuristic martial arts play held inside a locked, barbed-wire cage at the Joypolis Colosseum. The victim was struck on the back of the head with a heavy glass prop vase. Her co-star, Desireé DeLite, who had struck Yumi with the prop vase as part of the script rehearsal, panicked and left the cage to call an ambulance. Because the cage auto-locked from the outside and only the two actresses ostensibly had the specialized belts required to enter and exit it, Desireé DeLite was arrested as the sole suspect. 

---

## Background

### The Victim and the Play
Yumi was a celebrated actress cast in the leading role alongside Desireé DeLite. She had a severe phobia of the color red, which was so intense that experiencing a nosebleed during a previous rehearsal triggered a panic attack that left her paralyzed on the floor. Due to this phobia, the production completely omitted the use of fake blood. 

The play's script itself, conceptualized by Don, was notoriously bizarre. It culminated with a big-name actress being bludgeoned by a glowing vase, followed by a catfight where both parties smiled. 

### The Defendant
Desireé DeLite, Yumi's co-star, was earnestly rehearsing the climax of the play, which involved striking Yumi with a prop vase. When Yumi suddenly began bleeding from her arm, Desireé DeLite rushed out to call out for an ambulance, falling right into the true culprit's trap. She was initially represented by her husband, but Phoenix Wright took over her defense.

### The True Culprit and Accomplice
Don, the executive producer of the show and the imposing president of the "Don-ism" talent agency, viewed all aspects of life—including the judicial system—as variations of entertainment. Weighing 150 kg, he physically overpowered ordinary logic but possessed an ego to match. He enlisted the stage director, Sal Manella, to help him fabricate a witness testimony to frame Desireé DeLite.

## Investigation

Phoenix Wright investigated the "21st Century Champion" stage in Joypolis:

### The Ring (Crime Scene)
- Due to the high, spiked 30kg cage walls and auto-locking doors, Phoenix had to use the ceiling wire hoist system to enter the ring. 
- Yumi's body was at the center of the ring. A heavily shattered glass vase lay nearby, identified as the murder weapon. Because of its sturdy nature, it was highly unusual for the vase neck to be completely pulverized. 
- The victim’s belt, bearing Yumi's name on the back, was found discarded on the floor with wet marbled paint on it.
- A glowing fragment of glass, painted with luminescent paint, was hidden in the debris. 
- The ceiling wire was rated to hold a maximum limit of 140kg. 

### Detention Center
- Phoenix met with the defendant, Desireé DeLite, who insisted she only struck Yumi's *arm* during rehearsal, as Yumi always guarded her head with her left arm.
- She found it strange that Yumi's body was discovered near the cage's wire fence.
- Desireé elaborated on Yumi's malicious habit of spreading false rumors, and reiterated Yumi's intense hatred of the color red, which prevented Desireé from even wearing her signature red jacket around her.

### Dressing Room
- Phoenix spoke to Don, who confidently claimed that Desireé had intentionally swapped the prop vase for a real one before the rehearsal.
- Upon examining a costume rack, Phoenix discovered a **Missing Costume**—one of the victim's outfits was unaccounted for.
- Don also offhandedly mentioned the recent purchase of special water-based paint used to mix an original "marbled" color.

### Archive Room
- Gumshoe explained Yumi's extreme phobia of red, confirming no fake blood was supposed to be on set.
- A box of fake blood was found ignored in the back.
- Gumshoe noted that the luminescent vase had 5 alphabet letters painted on it. 
- Gumshoe humorously speculated that Don might be cursed, bringing murder to Joypolis everywhere he went.

## Trial — November 22nd, District Court No. 1

### Opening
Prosecutor Miles Edgeworth asserted that Desireé DeLite had confessed to striking the victim with a vase. Phoenix countered that Desireé DeLite hit Yumi with a *prop* vase during rehearsal, and that if she intended to murder Yumi, she would never have left to call an ambulance. Edgeworth brought forward Don as his prime eyewitness to prove the lethal strike was intentional. 

### Witness Testimony 1 — Don 
Don testified that he stood near the ring during the rehearsal and watched with his supposed "3.0 eyesight." He claimed there was no prop vase inside the ring, only the real one, and that no shattered fragments were on the ground when Yumi fell. 

**Cross-Examination:**
Phoenix presented the **Glowing Fragment**. A photograph of the scene clearly showed a piece of the luminescent prop vase glowing right by Don's feet. If Don's eyesight was as perfect as he claimed, he could not have possibly overlooked it—proving he lied about the state of the ring.

Don shamelessly pivoted, confessing that he had been lying and wasn’t actually watching the ring, but was doing office work nearby. He claimed nobody else entered the ring, and that the stage director, Sal Manella, had just been in the lighting room. 

**Phoenix's Rebuttal:**
Phoenix presented the **Missing Costume** to contradict this claim and demonstrate someone else was active on set, successfully summoning Stage Director Sal Manella to the stand.

### Witness Testimony 2 — Sal Manella
Manella claimed to have remained in the lighting room the entire time, having seen everything and never entering the ring himself to aid the victim. 

**Cross-Examination:**
Phoenix pointed out that Manella’s clothes were covered in marbled paint. He presented the **Victim's Belt**, which was found with the exact same wet marbled paint on it. It was impossible for Manella to have wet paint from the victim's belt unless he interacted with it. Phoenix deduced that Manella had called out to the paralyzed Yumi ("come over here") and taken her belt through the fence. Manella crumbled under the pressure, admitting that Don had coerced him to lie on the stand.

### The Final Showdown — Don Recalled
Don was brought back to the stand for a final testimony. Furious, he challenged Phoenix to prove he entered the cage. He argued that it was physically impossible to commit the crime: Don weighed 150 kg, while the ceiling wire hoist had a strict weight limit of 140 kg. Therefore, he could not have hoisted himself into the cage to murder Yumi. 

**The Missing Link:**
Phoenix presented the **Cage Fence**. The cage panels weighed 30 kg each. Phoenix deduced that Don never used the wire on *himself*. Instead, Don used the belt to hook the ceiling wire to the bottom of the cage fence. The 140 kg-capacity wire easily lifted the 30 kg fence, leaving the barbed wire slightly bent upwards. Don then slipped underneath the lifted fence to enter the cage, execute the murder, and slip back out. 

Cornered by the physical evidence of the bent wire and his entire plot laid bare, Don suffered a massive breakdown on the stand, cursing Phoenix and Edgeworth for crushing his "perfect entertainment."

## What Really Happened

Yumi had a malicious hobby of spreading false rumors to ruin the reputations of those around her, including selling fabricated stories about Desireé DeLite to magazines. Don, viewing human suffering and justice simply as another form of manipulable entertainment, decided to orchestrate an extravagant "locked room" murder. It served both to silence Yumi and perfectly frame Desireé in a theatrical display of his own "genius" as the mastermind of Joypolis. 

Don meticulously planned the set design: a 5-meter tall spiked cage that auto-locked and could only be entered via a specialized belt hooked to a 140kg-capacity ceiling wire. He coerced the stage director, Sal Manella, into assisting him. 

During the rehearsal, Desireé—knowing Yumi always guarded her head—intentionally struck Yumi's arm with the *prop* vase instead. Seeing the blood, Yumi suffered a severe panic attack due to her phobia of the color red and froze. Desireé immediately left the cage using her belt and the ceiling wire to call an ambulance. 

With Desireé gone, Sal Manella called out to the paralyzed Yumi ("come over here") and convinced her to pass her belt through the gaps in the fence. With Yumi's belt secured, Don attached the ceiling wire hoist to the bottom of the 30kg cage fence itself (since Don weighed 150kg and could not use the 140kg hoist on his own body). The wire lifted the cage wall, creating a gap underneath. Don slipped inside the ring, retrieved the real, heavy glass vase, and bludgeoned Yumi over the head with such extreme force that the thick glass neck completely shattered. He then slipped back under the fence and lowered the cage, leaving it perfectly auto-locked from the outside with no apparent way for anyone but Desireé to have committed the crime.

## Verdict

**Desireé DeLite — Not Guilty.**
