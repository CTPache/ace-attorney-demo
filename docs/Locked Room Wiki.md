'''''Turnabout Locked Room''''' is a case from the Joypolis ''Ace Attorney'' attraction. It features [[Phoenix Wright]] defending actor [[Will Powers]] after he is accused of fatally stabbing the Joypolis manager inside a locked office room.

==Oct. 15==
=== The crime ===
Joypolis facility manager [[Rando Daiba]] was murdered inside his secured office. Daiba, a massive fan of the "Jammin' Ninja" franchise, was stabbed in the back of the neck. 

The true culprit was Daiba's personal manager, [[Ini Miney]]. Knowing Daiba's bizarre dietary habit of putting soy sauce on virtually everything, she offered him a laced packet of soy sauce for his fried food bento. Daiba fell unconscious from the sleeping pills. While struggling to hide his heavy body inside a large wooden cabinet, Miney accidentally smashed Daiba's head against the door's carvings and chipped a piece of her own hat off in the scuffle. To hide herself before anyone arrived, she hastily disassembled Daiba's prized giant Jammin' Ninja figure to make room inside a locker, locking herself inside the room he occupied. Roughly 40 minutes later, she emerged from hiding to fatally stab Daiba and clumsily force the action figure back together, making immense snapping noises in the process.

===Arrest===
[[Will Powers]], an actor performing in the facility's zombie attraction and a fellow Jammin' Ninja fan, checked the manager's office at 7:15 p.m. to collect bento boxes. He locked the door with his master key when he left. Discovering the body upon his return at 8:00 p.m., he ran to get help. This brief window of panic allowed Miney to slip out. As Powers held the only master key capable of securing the room beforehand, he was swiftly arrested under the premise that he created the locked-room scenario.

===Investigation===
[[Phoenix Wright]] arrived alongside [[Maya Fey]] and [[Dick Gumshoe]] to investigate. 

Wright was briefed by Edgeworth and Gumshoe at the Crime Scene. They noted the lack of fingerprints on the murder weapon. Inspecting the room, Wright found Daiba's half-eaten bento and deduced a critical contradiction: although the catering company exclusively provided Worcestershire sauce, someone had poured both Worcestershire and soy sauce on the fried food-with sleeping pills found only in the soy sauce. Given Daiba's bizarre obsession with putting soy sauce on everything, the killer weaponized his tastes against him to ensure he'd willingly ingest the drugs. Wright also examined a large wooden cabinet locked with a 4-digit code. He found fresh scratches and an embedded plastic fragment on top of the cabinet doors. Daiba's bandana carried a yellow paint mark in a diamond shape that perfectly matched the cabinet's carved design, proving Daiba's head had violently struck the cabinet before his death.

In the Lobby, Gumshoe revealed a memo from the victim stating the locker code could be cracked via a "poster" in the room. Gumshoe also bitterly complained about narrowly missing out on the "Last One Award" gold-plated Ninja Nanja figure at a nearby candy store because he had to run to an ATM at around 7:40 p.m.

At the Cafeteria, Wright interrogated [[Ini Miney]], the victim's eccentric manager who claimed a "parapsychology god" directed her employment. Wearing a hat decorated with a plastic shuriken, Miney claimed she ate dinner with Daiba and watched him eat the soy-sauce-laced food before she left at 7:10 p.m. She then aggressively asserted she saw a "pale" Powers carrying Daiba's heavy body into the office at exactly 7:40 p.m.

Finally, Wright visited the Detention Center to speak with [[Will Powers]]. Powers explained his timeline: he donned his gruesome "Zombie Special Makeup" at 6:00 p.m. and hadn't taken it off. At 7:15 p.m., he checked the office to gather bentos but only saw Daiba's half-eaten food, so he assumed he stepped out and locked the door with his master key. He then confirmed the location of the candy store Gumshoe mentioned and revealed he was the one who pulled the "Last One Award" at exactly 7:40 p.m., much to Gumshoe's dismay, before returning to the office at 8:00 p.m. to discover the body.

==Oct. 16==
===Trial ===
The trial convened the next day in District Court No. 1. Prosecutor [[Miles Edgeworth]] argued that Powers committed the murder and used his master key to lock the room from the outside. 

[[Ini Miney]], the victim's eccentric assistant who claimed a "parapsychology god" directed her employment, took the stand. She falsely testified that she saw a "pale" Powers carrying Daiba's heavy body into the office at exactly 7:40 p.m., forcing him to use his key to hide the crime. Wright quickly dismantled her testimony on two fronts. First, he presented the "Zombie Special Makeup"—Powers was fully dressed as an undead zombie covered in thick fake blood from 6:00 p.m. onwards, making it impossible for anyone to see his face turn "pale." Second, Wright presented the rare gold-plated "Last One Award" Jammin' Ninja figure. Powers won this figure by pulling the final lottery ticket at a candy store ten minutes away at 7:40 p.m.—a flawless alibi corroborated by Detective Gumshoe, who had tragically just run to an ATM for money to buy that exact same ticket.

With Miney's timeline shattered, the trial shifted to determining who actually created the locked room. [[Wendy Oldbag]], a worker for the catering company, testified that she arrived at the locked office door at 7:50 p.m. to collect bentos and heard violent "snapping" and "breaking" sounds from inside. Wright explained these bizarre noises: they were the sounds of the true murderer hastily forcing the joints of the giant Jammin' Ninja figure back together after previously taking it apart to hide inside the locker alongside the unconscious Daiba.

The final contradiction lay in the wooden cabinet's scratches. Wright pointed out the plastic fragment embedded in the wood from the earlier struggle. He then presented Miney's hat, which featured a plastic shuriken decoration completely missing a piece that perfectly matched the fragment. Cornered by the undeniable physical evidence connecting her to a violent struggle with Daiba's unconscious body, Miney confessed, completely exonerating Powers.

===Aftermath ===
Will Powers was declared Not Guilty. The locked-room mystery was solved by proving the room wasn't locked to keep the killer out, but that the killer had hidden inside the room before it was secured by the defendant.
