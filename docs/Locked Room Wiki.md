## Incident Overview

On the evening of October 11th, Rando Daiba, the manager of Joypolis, was found dead in his office. The victim had been drugged with sleeping pills concealed in soy sauce and then stabbed in the back of the neck with a knife. At the time of discovery the office constituted a perfect locked room: the door was locked and the windows were secured from the inside. Only two keys to the office existed—the master key, which was on the victim's desk, and a single spare key held by Will Powers. Powers, who discovered the body at 8:00 PM, was arrested on the spot.

---

## Background

### The Victim and His World

Rando Daiba was a veteran action star turned Joypolis manager, well-liked by his staff. He was a hardcore collector of *Jammin' Ninja* merchandise; his prized possession—a super-rare giant buildable figure—was kept locked inside a cabinet in his office behind a 4-digit dial padlock. Despite guarding it carefully, he was generous enough to show it to anyone who asked.

Daiba was famously obsessed with soy sauce, pouring it on nearly everything he ate—even dishes that would normally call for Worcestershire sauce. He often joked that he wished he had been born into a soy sauce brewer's family.

### The Defendant

Will Powers was a member of the Joypolis theater troupe and a fellow *Jammin' Ninja* enthusiast who genuinely admired Daiba. He routinely handled tasks around the office, including collecting and distributing the troupe's catering bento boxes and cleaning the manager's office—which is why he held the only spare key. Powers had been in occasional salary disputes with Daiba, a fact the prosecution would later exploit as motive.

### The True Culprit

Ini Miney worked as Daiba's personal manager after claiming to have been divinely guided by "the god of parapsychology" to seek employment under him. Despite her outwardly ditzy demeanor, she harbored an unrequited obsession with Daiba, growing resentful that he never paid her the personal attention she craved.

## Investigation

Phoenix Wright's investigation took him to four key locations:

### Crime Scene — Manager's Office
- The victim was found with a knife in his neck; no fingerprints on the weapon.
- A half-eaten fried-food bento sat on the table alongside the master key. The bento's fried food had **both** soy sauce and Worcestershire sauce on it—despite the catering company confirming that the bento only came with Worcestershire sauce.
- The wooden cabinet featured ornate yellow diamond carvings. Two scratches were found on the upper part of its doors, one with a mysterious plastic fragment embedded in it.
- A narrow locker stood empty—large enough for one adult to stand in.
- The padlock on the cabinet was a 4-digit combination lock. A memo in the victim's notebook read: *"If I make it the answer from the poster, I won't forget the 4-digit number."*

### Joypolis Entrance — Lobby
- Gumshoe shared the padlock notebook memo and the story of his own visit to the candy store, inadvertently providing key alibi evidence for the defendant.

### Break Room — Ini Miney's Testimony
- Ini claimed she and Daiba ate dinner together in the office and left together at 7:10 PM.
- She claimed to have seen Powers carry Daiba into the office at 7:40 PM and leave alone at 8:00 PM, looking "pale and shaken."
- She mentioned a *Jammin' Ninja* poster somewhere in Joypolis that reveals four numbers when solved—a clue to the cabinet padlock code.

### Detention Center — Will Powers's Account
- Powers explained his zombie makeup (applied since ~6:00 PM), his bento delivery routine, finding the office empty at 7:15 PM, and his unauthorized trip to the candy store.
- He produced the gold-plated *Last One Award* figure he won at ~7:40 PM.
- He adamantly denied any involvement and had no knowledge of the soy sauce packet found in his locker.

---

## Trial — October 12th, District Court No. 3

### Opening

Edgeworth laid out the prosecution's case: Powers drugged the victim via soy sauce laced with sleeping pills, stabbed him, and was the only person who could have accessed the locked room thanks to his spare key.

### Witness Testimony 1 — Ini Miney

Ini testified that she saw Powers carry Daiba into the office and later emerge alone, looking pale and panicked, locking the door behind him.

**Cross-Examination — Zombie Makeup Contradiction:**
Phoenix pointed out that Powers had been wearing heavy, blood-soaked zombie makeup since 6:00 PM. Ini could not possibly have seen him looking "pale"—his face was unrecognizable. Ini backpedaled, admitting she never actually saw his face but insisted it was Powers based on his blond hair and amended her timeline from 7:40 PM to 7:20 PM.

**Presenting the Candy Store Alibi:**
Phoenix presented the *Last One Award* figure. Powers testified he was at the candy store from 7:30–7:50 PM. Edgeworth dismissed this as unverifiable—the candy store owner had declined to testify due to her forgetfulness, and no security cameras existed.

Phoenix then called on Gumshoe, who confirmed that the store owner specifically remembered the last ticket being drawn by **a zombie**. Since only troupe actors wore zombie makeup and they were forbidden from leaving Joypolis, the zombie at the candy store could only have been Powers. The alibi was accepted.

### Witness Testimony 2 — Wendy Oldbag

The caterer testified she arrived at the office at 7:50 PM to collect bento boxes, found one missing, knocked on the locked door with no response, and heard "a sound like chopsticks being snapped apart" from inside.

**Cross-Examination — The Figure:**
Phoenix demonstrated that the sound was actually the Jammin' Ninja figure's joints being snapped. The figure in the cabinet had cracked joints and a backwards-attached shuriken—clear signs of amateur handling. A true fanatic like Powers would never damage the figure or misplace its parts. The person inside the room was someone unfamiliar with the Jammin' Ninja.

**Who Could Enter the Room?**
Edgeworth argued that only Powers had a key, so only he could have been inside. Phoenix countered: **Ini Miney** never actually left the room. If she lied about departing at 7:10 PM and instead remained inside, she would not have needed a key at all.

### Witness Testimony 3 — Ini Miney (Dinner Details)

Under re-examination, Ini testified that Daiba's bento originally contained **both** soy sauce and Worcestershire sauce packets. Phoenix pounced:

- If Daiba was a devoted soy sauce lover who always chose soy sauce when available, why did the victim's half-eaten bento have **Worcestershire sauce** on the fried food at all?
- Because the bento **only came with Worcestershire sauce**. Daiba used it reluctantly in the absence of soy sauce.
- Ini then arrived and handed him the drugged soy sauce packet—hence **both** condiments ended up on the food.

This proved Ini was the one who administered the sleeping pills.

### The Locked Room Solved

Phoenix laid out the full reconstruction:

1. Ini drugged Daiba during dinner, then hid his unconscious body in the cabinet.
2. She disassembled the buildable figure to fit Daiba inside the cabinet, and hid herself (along with the figure pieces) in the narrow locker.
3. When Powers checked the room at 7:15 PM, it appeared empty.
4. After Powers left and locked the door, Ini emerged, removed Daiba from the cabinet, and murdered him.
5. Under time pressure (Powers would return at 8:00 PM), she hastily reassembled the figure—cracking joints and misplacing the shuriken. This produced the "chopstick-snapping" sound Oldbag overheard.

### The Final Proof

Edgeworth demanded physical evidence. Phoenix directed the court's attention to the cabinet's two scratches:

- **Scratch 1:** Made by the victim's head when Ini lost her balance while stuffing Daiba into the cabinet. This explained the lump on Daiba's head and the diamond-pattern imprint on his bandana.
- **Scratch 2:** Made by Ini's own forehead hitting the cabinet door. No zombie makeup residue was found (ruling out Powers), but a **plastic fragment** was embedded in the scratch.

Maya noticed that one blade of the *Jammin' Ninja* shuriken on Ini's hat was chipped. Phoenix identified the embedded fragment as **the missing piece of Ini's shuriken**, broken off when her head struck the cabinet.

Confronted with irrefutable physical evidence tying her to the crime scene, Ini Miney broke down and confessed.

---

## Motive and Confession

Ini revealed that despite her devotion as Daiba's manager, he never reciprocated her personal feelings. Consumed by resentment, she devised the elaborate locked-room murder plan. She specifically chose to frame Powers because:

1. He and Daiba had a known salary dispute—providing plausible motive.
2. Powers did not carry a cell phone—meaning he would have to **leave the room** to report the body, giving Ini the window she needed to escape and plant the incriminating soy sauce packet in his locker.

After the verdict, Ini muttered to Powers: *"I wanted to be like you."* Phoenix interpreted this as Ini's envy of the genuine friendship Powers shared with Daiba—a closeness she had desperately wanted for herself.

Ini left a message through Edgeworth, apologizing to Powers for dragging him into her crime, and expressing her resolve to become someone capable of building the kind of meaningful relationship she had envied—thanking Phoenix for helping her realize that.

## Verdict

**Will Powers — Not Guilty.**
